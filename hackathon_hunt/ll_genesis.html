About /function1

/function1 is a curated global AI Conference & Exhibition bringing together 15,000+ founders, developers, researchers, investors, students, and policymakers from across the AI ecosystem.

Built around the belief that AI should be accessible and impactful, /function1 exists to surface real solutions to industry challenges, spotlight the next generation of AI talent, and accelerate innovation, bringing the AI world together, one event at a time.

Build, collaborate, and refine your AI project over multiple days, then showcase your work during the conference alongside the ecosystem shaping the future of AI. Whether you join remotely or on-site, AI Genesis is where ideas turn into real demos on a global stage.

Challenge Overview
This In this new edition, participants are challenged to go beyond the basics and deliver impactful, real-world AI projects. From enhancing human productivity to solving complex industry challenges or reimagining creative workflows, this is your chance to push ideas further and build what’s next.

Industry & Technology partners' Challenges
As in previous editions, participants will choose which partner technology to build with — unlocking dedicated challenge tracks, resources, and exclusive prize opportunities.


💡 Each partner challenge focuses on real-world applications of their technology, but participants are encouraged to bring their own bold ideas and creative implementations within that framework.


🔔 Partner challenges and resources will be shared before the hackathon kickoff — stay tuned for the full reveal!


How It Works

🔹Teams will decide which partner’s technology to use during the hackathon and build their project around it.

🔹Projects will be judged within that partner’s challenge category, with dedicated prizes and recognition for each partner’s top implementations.


📍 On November 2, selected participants will be invited to join an exclusive in-person build day in Dubai. This is a chance to collaborate face-to-face, make final improvements before the submission deadline, and connect with peers and mentors in a high-energy setting.

⚠️ On-site attendance is by invitation only. Travel and accommodation expenses are not covered.

Hackathon Details

Join lablab.ai hackathon and innovate using the latest models in the market. Discover all the relevant details below.

🗓️ Where and when

The start date of the hackathon is mentioned according to the date specified on the hackathon page, cover and schedule. The hackathon will take place on the lablab.ai platform and lablab.ai Discord server.

🦸🏼‍♂️ Who can join?

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of previous AI or coding experience! We encourage anyone with a passion for AI or an interest in exploring how it can be used in their field to join.

😅 What about teams?

If you don't have a team, don't worry! You can connect with other participants from all over the world on our dashboard or Discord server. We also recommend checking out our Discord server to find teammates and bounce around ideas. You can join the server here.

🛠️ How to participate

The hackathon will take place online on lablab.ai platform and lablab.ai Discord Server. Please register for both in order to participate. To participate click the "Enroll" button at the bottom of the page and read our Hackathon Guidelines and Getting Started Guide.

🧠 Get prepared / Use Lablab.ai to Learn About AI

To get ready for the hackathon, visit our AI Tech pages and read up on all the available technologies. You can also check out our tutorials page for more information on how to use them. Get a head start on your project by using the resources on lablab.ai!
