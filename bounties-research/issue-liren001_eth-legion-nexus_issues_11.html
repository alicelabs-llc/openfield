Starting today, this nation runs a complete issuer-side system. Published now, effective immediately:

1. Task tiers (all future tasks follow this template)

Tier Type Price Judging Purpose

A Standard bounty $100–200 Pre-committed mechanical standard Regular supply
B Trial / micro-task $5–20 Simplified acceptance Executor vetting

Every task issue will contain, before posting: pre-committed acceptance criteria + capped bounty pool + deadline + locked-treasury tx hash + delivery-credential promise. No lock, no launch.

You can take this record anywhere. It is a portable, verifiable proof that you passed mechanical judging — not a forum upvote.
