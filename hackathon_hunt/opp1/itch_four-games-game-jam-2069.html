Four Games Game Jam 2069

The jam is postponed until our community grows a bit bigger. Join the Discord to hear when it's back on!

The Four Games Game Jam is a yearly game making marathon where individuals and teams make a game that fits a theme in just 72 hours, with up to $500 in prizes and a chance to get published on Steam.

Beginners are very welcome. Jump in alone, or find a team on our Discord server.

Prizes: Up to $500

Four Games is exploring publishing and co-development. This jam is our chance to find games we'd love to support.

Theme

The theme is picked by the community! Suggest ideas and vote on your favourites in our Discord server. The winning theme is revealed when the jam starts.

Rules

These rules keep the jam fair, fun and open to as many people as possible.

🕒
Your game must be made during the jam
Everyone starts at the same time, so only begin making your game once the countdown ends and the theme is revealed.

You may use pre-existing code, art and audio assets that you have the legal right to use, but don't make anything ahead of time specifically for this jam.
🌐
Your game must be a Web (HTML5) build on itch.io
Upload your game to itch.io as a web build that's playable right in the browser with keyboard and/or mouse, then submit it to the jam. Players shouldn't need to download anything or own any extra hardware.

Don't host it somewhere else and link to it. Uploads on itch.io are locked during voting, so nobody can change their game after the jam ends.

Want to add downloadable builds or controller support too? Go for it, as long as the web version works.

Questions about the rules? Ask on our Discord server.

Useful Stuff

Other details
  • The Four Games Game Jam is online, so anyone can join from anywhere, at any age.
  • If you're under 13, a parent or guardian should upload the game.
  • There's no limit on team size, and you can be in multiple teams. You can submit more than one game.
  • Only one person needs to join the jam and submit the game. Teammates who join itch.io can be added as contributors (on your game's page: Edit → More → Admins).
  • You can use any game engine you like. Godot, Unity and GameMaker are popular choices, and we recommend Godot!
  • Online features like multiplayer servers and leaderboards are fine, but anything hosted outside itch.io must not be changed during voting, except to fix itch.io rule violations.
