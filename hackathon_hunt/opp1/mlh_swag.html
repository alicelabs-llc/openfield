Swag

There is plenty to be excited about at Global Hack Week. We want to make sure you have a chance to rep Global Hack Week long after the week ends. To earn our basic swag, which includes a sticker pack and postcard, make sure you've checked into the event, which includes joining the MLH Community Discord Server, submitting an address at mlh.link/Address, and completing all of our registration challenges. To earn our bonus swag (which includes extra stickers!) make sure you attend at least 5 live sessions at GHW. Take a look below at some of the items you can earn at GHW.
