That's right! We're back for our second game jam! Join us as we attempt to host a nice and slightly spooky jam with absolutely no surprises and no theme clashing. Scout's honor.



Running the week of Oct. 4th-10th, this game jam will be unlike any you've done before (or will do)(unless you participated in the first one). You'll have ONE WEEK to make a game using the prompt/theme given. The twist? The week before (Sept 29th-Oct 1st ) you'll vote on a new modifier for your game! You'll vote each day on three required mechanics and whichever one wins the day is the one that YOU decide to vote for! That's right, you can choose which modifier you get, but it has to win the majority vote! 

And on the 11th when all is said and done, tune in at 12PM EST to a special livestream hosted on our YouTube as we play through the submissions and rank them. Whichever dev wins the best community and judge ranking, wins a $100 Steam Gift Card or $100 towards their Steam page. So mark your calendars, you won't want to miss this.

The game mechanics/modifiers are as follows:

MODIFIER 1: SETTINGS NOT FIT FOR HORROR

MODIFIER 2: SACRIFICE MECHANICS 

MODIFIER 3: RIDDLES

these will need to be worked in along with the theme for your jam submission. It's up to you to interpret what those mean! Have fun and be creative with it!


THE GHOST IN THE MACHINE



>Explicit content: Allowed but tag your game as NSFW (NSFW games will not be streamed due to ToS)

>Using IRL images of people who are not you will result in immediate disqualification.

>No late submissions. All games must be submitted by 1AM EST Oct 10th

>Voting happens Oct. 10th-11th

>Games will be scored based on community ranking and our judge’s (BrimstoneWalker) ranking, highest combined score wins

>Winner must provide contact info of some kind (email, discord, etc.) through our email gamesubmit4v3@gmail.com

>DISCLAIMER: We discourage the usage of AI for art. It will knock down your overall score.
