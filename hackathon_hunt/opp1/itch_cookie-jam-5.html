Cookie Jam is a jam made for everyone! Whether you like cookies or not.
Check out the discord server!

◝(ᵔᵕᵔ)◜
This cookie jam has a special challenge, your game could include a cookie in any way.
It maybe the sound effect, music, an enemy or just the main player.
THIS IS NOT REQUIRED but it would be fun :D

Theme : LAZY
"I'm too lazy to find a quote." ~ Me

Lazy can refer to procastination, tired etc
Like, take a scene of just hanging out in the fall season,
Or doing procrastination and trying to make yourself stop

This theme is open for interpretation

Be sure to give your progress in the discord server!
