    Digiclub Project

    Empowering Digital Football Clubs’ Global Presence

    Our goal is simple yet ambitious:

    To equip football clubs with the tools and platform necessary to unlock their full potential in the digital age.

    The Digiclub project continues the initiative of the Shuttleworth Foundation, aiming to promote diversity and equality in sports by providing digital representation to football clubs that are yet to establish a strong online presence.While the Digiclub project initially launches in Africa, we aspire to support clubs worldwide that lack full digital representation, aligning with the demands of the 21st century.
    Together, We Will Create Your Ideal Website

    To enhance online presence of your football club we will present the following support:

    Full Website Creation: Simply send us your content aligned with the Digiclub Kit, and within a week, we'll have your website up and running

    Website Structure: We've conducted research on the best practices of world football club websites, providing structured framework for your website.

    Free Domain Support, Maintenance and Hosting: Digiclub covers all costs associated with domein support, maintenance, hosting, and error correction, relieving clubs of financial burdens associated with managing an online presence independently.

    How Will a Website Benefit My Football Club?
      1. Global Visibility: Having your own website will allow you to attract more users locally and globally, expanding your reach and tapping into the global professional community.
      2. Professional Recognition: A well-designed and maintained website reflects positively on the professionalism and credibility of a football club, enhancing its reputation.
      3. Enhanced Fan Engagement: Interactive features like news updates, match statistics, and feedback channels deepen fan engagement, fostering a stronger sense of community and loyalty.

        Overall, the website will host and structure detailed information about players, coaches, training schedules, etc., making it easy for potential partners and fans to find the information they need.
    Next Steps

    Here what would be our next steps:


    1. Onboarding: We'll schedule an introduction meeting to present our project, discuss your future website structure, and personalize solutions if needed. We'll also discuss the content required for your website and address any questions you may have.
    2. Signing the Memorandum of Understanding: Once we've discussed the project details, we'll proceed with signing the memorandum to formalize our partnership and commence our collaboration.
    3. Content Integration: Together, we'll collect and prepare all the necessary content for your website.
    4. Launch: Once we have all the required content and confirm the domain name, we'll present the ready-to-go website for your approval. If everything looks good, we'll launch it the next day.
    5. Ongoing Support: We'll work together to establish a process for updating the website with the latest news and content. Additionally, we'll create a platform that allows you to manage updates yourself, with our ongoing assistance whenever needed.

    How else will Digiclub project support you?

    • Promotion Assistance: Upon launching your website, we'll leverage our resources and media channels to enhance the reach of your club, amplifying its visibility and impact.



    • Content Collaboration: We'll work together to establish a process for updating the website with your latest news and content, thinking together how to evolve and grow.



    • Data and Insights: Access to data and insights on fan behavior, website traffic, and engagement metrics from your website will drive informed decisions and optimize digital strategies.
