Funding a better tech future

Mozilla Foundation funds changemakers who refuse to accept the status quo in tech, and they’re building better.

The future is still ours to build.

Right now, a handful of companies are building the technology the rest of us live with, from the apps on our phones to the AI showing up in clinics, classrooms, and city halls. Their choices decide who benefits, whose languages get spoken, and what it all costs our planet and our privacy. And those choices are quickly becoming the infrastructure we’ll all be living inside for decades.

But none of this is locked in yet. Investing in alternatives now, while these technologies are still taking shape, can keep a few companies’ choices from becoming everyone’s defaults.

Mozilla Foundation funds the changemakers who believe there’s a better way, and then go build it. They’re educators teaching students how to create technology differently, builders making tools sized for the communities they serve, and artists showing us what technology can be when it’s made to serve people.

We know what it takes for better alternatives to thrive.

Almost thirty years ago, Mozilla took on a one-size-fits-all browser with open-source code and a community of people who believed the web should belong to everyone. That fight taught us what it takes for an alternative to last: patient funding, hands-on support, and a community that shows up when things get hard. Our grantmaking is designed to provide all three because we know that’s what better tech needs to thrive.

Over the last decade, Mozilla Foundation has invested more than $35 million in people and projects building a better technology future.

We make these investments because the technology that serves people best is built differently. It’s built closer to the people it affects, it’s accountable to the communities it serves, and it’s open enough that others can build on it. That kind of work rarely gets early backing from markets that reward speed and scale, so we step in early and stay through the stretch where most promising ideas run out of runway.

Find where you work fits

There is no single path to a better technology future. It takes leaders who can move the field, educators changing how technology is taught, partners who can move resources where they matter, and builders creating alternatives for their communities. We fund, you build.

But you never have to do it alone. A grant from Mozilla Foundation comes with people attached. You’ll get coaching from folks who’ve built products, organizations, and movements, and you’ll meet peers across the world who are wrestling with the same problems you are. At gatherings like MozFest, you’ll swap ideas with builders, educators, and artists from across the globe and leave with new partners and possibilities. The funding helps you get started, but it’s the community that keeps you building long after the grant ends.
