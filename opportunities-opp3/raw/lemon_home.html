Questions and answers

What is Lemon.io?

Lemon.io is a platform that connects businesses with top freelance developers. It helps companies find skilled professionals for their projects, ensuring quality and efficiency in the hiring process.

How are you different from the other freelance platforms?

We pre-vet every engineer and hand-match them to your stack — no endless profiles to sift through.

Is there a minimum number of project hours?

Yes, 160 hours. That’s one month full-time (40 hours a week) or two months part-time (20 hours a week).

Is there a minimum project duration?

There is no separate minimum duration. A 160-hour full-time project typically takes about four weeks; part-time engagements run longer.

Do I own the IP?

Yes. You own all intellectual property produced for your project.

Will I get a project estimation before the start?

Yes. You will get a clear estimate before any work begins.

What technologies do you work with?

Full-stack, front-end, back-end, mobile, AI and DevOps across 50+ technologies.

Are there any upfront fees?

There are no upfront recruitment or setup fees. However, Lemon.io operates on a prepaid subscription model, so you pay for the developer’s working hours ahead of time. Payment is typically made one month in advance, though this depends on your billing arrangement.
