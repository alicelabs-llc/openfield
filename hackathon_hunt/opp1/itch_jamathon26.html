A 48-Hour Game Jam About Mental Health

Build a game that changes how people understand serious mental health conditions, and improves the lives of those impacted. Open to writers, artists, coders, composers, people with lived experience, and first-time jammers.

$15,000

In Cash Prizes

The Essentials

When   Sat Sept 26 to Sun Sept 27, 2026  (Sat 9 AM to Sun 9 PM)

Where   Clark University, 7 Hawthorne St, Worcester, MA (in person)

Team size   2 to 6 people (come as a team or find one on Saturday)

Who   Open to everyone 18+, every skill level

Sign up   discord.gg/jCHVrwVF2a

The Challenge

Games matter. Can a game reduce loneliness, build empathy, and support recovery?

Over one weekend, interdisciplinary teams design and prototype games of every kind (board, card, and video) that reshape how people understand serious mental health conditions. You won't be doing it from the outside: mentors with lived experience, clinicians, and researchers work right alongside you.

What to Expect

Mentors in the room: office hours all weekend with game designers, clinicians, researchers, and people with lived experience.

Any kind of game: board, card, or digital. The format is yours; the goal is connection, reduced isolation, and recovery.

Showcase & judging: present your prototype to judges and the community on Sunday.

Fed the whole time: Chipotle, pizza, midnight donuts, bagels, snack rounds, and a catered finale.

First-timers welcome: a rules read-through and mentor intros get everyone started together.

Find your team: arrive with a crew or form one during Saturday's team finalization and mingling.

Before You Sign Up

What does it cost?

Nothing. The jam is free to enter, and food is provided all weekend.

Do I need experience or a team?

Neither. All skill levels are welcome, and you can form a team on Saturday.

Does it have to be a video game?

No. Board, card, and video games all count.

Do I have to attend in person?

Yes, participants take part on-site at Clark University. Only mentors may join virtually.

How do I sign up?

In our Discord: discord.gg/jCHVrwVF2a
