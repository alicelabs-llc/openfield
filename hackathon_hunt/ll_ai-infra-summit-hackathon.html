The official hackathon of AI Infra Summit — co-organized by Kisaco Research and lablab.ai.


Build production-grade AI applications on the modern AI infrastructure stack, alongside 8,000+ attendees, researchers, founders, and infra leaders.

How to Join

On-site

Santa Clara
Important: Create your team on lablab.ai and add your project idea as early as possible. We use your idea to match and allocate your team to the most suitable on-site track, so make sure it's included in both your Team Description and the Confirm & Apply for Track Form.
  1. Sign up

    Register on the Event Page and select that you're interested in joining on-site.

  2. Create or join a team

    Everyone needs a team on lablab.ai to submit a project. Teams can have 1–5 members. Hacking solo? Create a one-person team. Create your team as early as possible. You can change your Team Name and Description at any time.

  3. Check your inbox

    We'll email interested participants with a Confirm & Apply for Track Form. Check your spam folder too!

  4. Confirm & apply for a track

    Fill out the form to confirm your on-site participation. Before submitting:

    • Add your project idea as your Team Description. Make it clear what you want to build and which partner challenge it addresses.
    • Team Leaders: select your team's track preferences. You can find all track descriptions here on the Event Page under the Tracks section.

We'll review your project idea and preferences, assign your team to an on-site track, and email you with the next steps.

If you have any questions or need to update anything, contact us at [email protected].

Online

Join from anywhere
  1. Sign up

    Register for the hackathon on the Event Page.

  2. Create or join a team

    Everyone needs a team on lablab.ai to submit a project. Teams can have 1–5 members. Hacking solo? Create a one-person team. Create your team as early as possible. You can change your Team Name and Description at any time.

  3. Start hacking

    Join the online kickoff on September 10 and start building.

  4. Submit your project

    Submit your project on lablab.ai before the deadline and select the online track you built for. Each project can be submitted to one track only.

AI Infra Summit Hackathon

Choose Your Challenge Track

Build applications that demonstrate the power of modern AI infrastructure. Every project at the AI Infra Summit Hackathon is built inside a partner-led Challenge Track — you pick a track, you get the technology behind it, and you build with the team that made it. Onsite tracks require assignment ahead of time; online tracks are open build. 🚀

Track Selection Process:

1

Create Your Team. Form your team as soon as possible. Onsite track assignment is only made for participants who are part of a team. Head over to the lablab.ai Discord server to connect with others — we'll also run Team Match-Making rounds ahead of the hackathon.

2

Submit Your Team's Onsite Track Preference. Only required for teams pursuing an onsite track. Team leaders submit the Track Selection Form on behalf of the team, listing all members and their preferred onsite track. Each team will receive confirmation of their assigned track.

3

One Onsite Track Per Team. Teams building an onsite challenge may only be assigned to one onsite track. Online tracks don't require assignment — build with any online track's technology directly.

4

Onsite Eligibility. Only teams working within their assigned onsite track are eligible for onsite participation at the AI Infra Summit.

⚠️ TBD — exact onsite capacity, the Track Selection Form link, and the submission deadline are still being finalized and will be communicated before the hackathon.

Requires Assignment

Physical AI Challenge

Robotic defect detection, VLA reasoning, and optimized on-device execution

Onsite Intermediate–Advanced SO-101 arm provided

Build an end-to-end Physical AI solution in which an SO-101 robotic arm performs autonomous defect detection and the appropriate physical response. Teams will use Intel Physical AI Studio to design the Vision-Language-Action (VLA) workflow, Anomalib for visual anomaly and defect detection, and OpenVINO to optimize and deploy the pipeline on an Intel Core Ultra Series 3 processor.

SO-101 Robotic Arm Intel Physical AI Studio Anomalib OpenVINO Intel Core Ultra Series 3

Developer resources

Intel's Hack-a-thon Resources page documents the exact stack you'll be working in: Physical AI Studio, OpenVINO 2026.3, Anomalib v2.6.0 and LeRobot on PyTorch XPU, running on Ubuntu 24.04 with the Intel NPU and iGPU drivers. It covers how to launch Physical AI Studio, how to verify every component with the included verify_stack.py script, and a troubleshooting table for the errors that come up most often.

Build Physical AI That Sees, Understands, and Acts

An open brief on SiMa.ai's Modalix MLSoC DevKit and Palette Neat

Onsite Modalix DevKit per team SiMa.ai mentors on site

In this in-person hackathon, participants will use SiMa.ai's Modalix MLSoC Development Kit and Palette Neat agentic development environment to build a working Physical AI application and explore the "art of the possible" with SiMa.ai technology.

Bonus Award

Open To Every Track

Best Use of Speechmatics

Build something people talk to — add it to whichever track you're building in

Bonus Award Onsite + Online No assignment needed

Build something people talk to. We're looking for agents that listen, understand and act on real speech in real time.

This one isn't a track — it's an award on top of the track you're already building in. Add Speechmatics to your project, whichever Challenge Track you picked, and you compete in two categories at once: your track's prizes and Best Use of Speechmatics. Open to onsite and online teams alike, with no separate sign-up and no extra track assignment.

Speechmatics provides state-of-the-art speech-to-text and voice AI, helping teams add accurate transcription and understanding to agents, workflows, and real-time applications.

Build with it

  • Add real-time or batch speech-to-text to agent workflows
  • Build voice-driven automation, transcription, and analysis tools
  • Power multilingual or accent-diverse voice applications
  • Close the loop with text-to-speech and the Voice Agents SDK
  • Drop it into a LiveKit, Pipecat or Vapi pipeline instead of wiring speech yourself

Pick a model

Three speech-to-text models, one API. Whichever you start with, the config is a single model property — so switching mid-build costs you nothing.

  • Enhanced — highest accuracy, Realtime and Batch, with a medical variant for healthcare audio
  • Standard — fastest turnaround when latency and throughput matter more than maximum accuracy, Realtime and Batch
  • Melia 1 — automatic multilingual transcription for audio where speakers switch language mid-conversation; Batch only, and a reduced feature set while it's in early access

Guidelines

Guidelines

Where and when

The hackathon runs September 10–17, 2026. The online build takes place on the lablab.ai platform and the lablab.ai Discord server. The on-site phase is held at the Santa Clara Convention Center during AI Infra Summit, September 15–17.

On-site participation is by invitation only. Travel and accommodation expenses will not be covered.

How to participate

Register on both the lablab.ai platform and the lablab.ai Discord server in order to participate. To join, click the Enroll button at the bottom of the page and read the Hackathon Guidelines, the Getting Started Guide and the lablab.ai Hackathon Rule Book.

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of previous AI or coding experience.

Before the kickoff, browse the AI Tech and tutorials pages to read up on the available technologies and get a head start on your project.

Teams

Teams consist of 1–5 people. If you don't have a team, don't worry. You can connect with other participants from all over the world on the dashboard or on the Discord server, where you can also find teammates and bounce around ideas.

What to submit

  • Long description
  • Technology & category tags

📸 Cover image and presentation

  • Cover image
  • Video presentation
  • Slide presentation

💻 App hosting and repository

  • Public GitHub repository
  • Demo application platform
  • Application URL

Judging criteria

Application of Technology

How effectively the chosen model(s) are integrated into the solution.

Presentation

The clarity and effectiveness of the project presentation.

Business Value

The impact and practical value, considering how well it fits into business areas.

Originality

The uniqueness and creativity of the solution, highlighting approaches and ability to demonstrate behaviors.

Partner tracks are scored against their own published rubric in addition to these criteria — see the Hackathon Tracks section.
