FAQ

Questions

I joined in September. What happens to my points?

Every point you earned stays. Your total carries over 1:1 and nobody loses a rank.

Can I join now?

Yes. Join any day. Courses and invites score from day one, and the cash goes to the top 3 by total points, so there is no head start that locks you out.

Do I need my own AMD hardware or cloud credits?

No. You do not need your own AMD hardware, AMD Developer Cloud credits, or Fireworks API credits to take part in a Match. GPU infrastructure is provided through the hackathon notebook environment.

How do I access the GPU infrastructure?

Go to notebooks.amd.com/hackathon and sign in with your AMD Developer Program SSO credentials. Press “Launch Notebook” to start a JupyterLab session on a GPU node, wait for it to become ready, then open it. The first redirected session URL is single-use and short-lived, so each time you return, open a fresh tab and start again from notebooks.amd.com/hackathon instead of bookmarking the redirected URL.

How does the daily GPU time quota work?

GPU sessions are capped at 3 hours of running time per day, and the quota refreshes every 24 hours. An idle session still consumes your quota because the timer measures how long the pod exists, not how long you are actively typing. When you stop working, press “Turn-off Session” so you do not use quota unnecessarily.

Where should I save my files so they persist?

Each user gets around 25 GB of persistent storage. Check the name in your redirected notebook URL before saving: if it contains “jupyter-hack-***”, save persistent files under /persistent — files under /workspace will not survive quota exhaustion or session shutdown. If the URL contains “rgapi-hackathon-***”, save persistent files under /workspace — files stored elsewhere will not persist. Only files within the correct persistent location, up to about 25 GB, survive when the session is turned off or the quota is exhausted.

I'm a beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Start with Onboarding and the Academy courses. Matches get more technical as the League progresses, while the Academy courses help you build the skills to take them on.

How do I get AMD certified?

The League highlights ROCm™ Certified Associate as the main AMD credential. Complete the certification requirements to earn the credential and add it to your professional profile.

How do Referral Leaders work?

Share your referral link. A friend counts once they join and finish at least one AMD AI Academy course. The 3 builders with the most qualified friends on Dec 1 win the referral prizes. Your friends also earn you points on the main leaderboard.

I missed a match. Can I still do it?

Yes. Closed Matches remain playable for points only. Prizes go only to submissions made while the Match is live.

Can I still earn points after the final Match ends?

Yes. The final Match ends on Nov 14, but the League continues through Dec 1. You can keep earning points through AMD AI Academy courses, referrals, open-source projects, approved blogs or tutorials, League progress posts and eligible Discord activity until the League concludes.

Can I have more than one account or refer myself?

No. One account per person. Self-referrals and fake accounts are removed with their points.

Can I play in a team?

No. Individual participation only. Every builder earns points on their own account.
