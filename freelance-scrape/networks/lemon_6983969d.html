How it works

From application to approval in days

No back and forth scheduling, unnecessary steps, or coding marathons. Know exactly where you stand at each stage, and talk to real engineers who make the final call.

  1. 1

    Share your info

    Upload your CV and LinkedIn link to create your Lemon profile quickly. Then, choose a suitable role from options like “full-stack, Python/React” or “backend, Node.js, PostgreSQL” to select your technical assessments.

  2. 2

    Schedule a call

    In 20 minutes or less, our AI assistant Mark confirms your experience, availability, time zone, rates, and the kinds of projects you want. It’s audio only, so you can take the call from your couch, your commute, wherever.

Frequently asked questions

You’re thorough, and so are we.
Here’s what to know about working with us.

How do you differ from a regular outstaffing company or a freelance platform?

Once you join Lemon’s network, you’re done pitching. We know your skills, pricing, and availability, and match you to best-fit opportunities as soon as they come in. You decide which projects to accept and work directly with the client team – no middleman involved.

Unlike freelancing, where you’re not 100% sure what you’re getting into before signing on the dotted line, Lemon only works with clients who offer stable, long-term projects, pay on time, and set clear requirements.

Who are your clients?

Mid-sized companies and startups in the U.S. and Western Europe that want to ship new features as quickly as possible.

Many are working on AI-driven products or adding AI to core workflows and face the same challenges teams everywhere are trying to solve. Successfully navigating these problems makes you invaluable across industries, not just the one you’re working in now.

And you’ll get hands-on experience from day one. Our clients want embedded team members who join standups, contribute to design decisions, update prompt libraries, review PRs, and debug production issues.

What kind of work relationship will we have?

You work as an independent contractor, not a Lemon employee. Choose to collaborate with us as an individual or through your own registered entity if you have one.

We handle all the behind-the-scenes negotiations with clients and check in with you periodically to make sure you’re still liking your work. If something’s off, we step in to resolve it.

Which payment options are available?

Direct to your bank account, or through Payoneer or Wise.

How do I find projects after onboarding?

Keep your Slack notifications on. We send you opportunities that best match your skills and rate.

You can also browse open projects on the platform to see what clients are building (and what new skills you may need to develop), but it’s just for visibility. We don’t do bidding.

How soon will I get my first project?

It depends on your skills, availability, and rate. If you’re ready for full-time work, your skills are in high demand, and clients are willing to pay your rate, you could get a few offers within days of onboarding with Lemon.

Can I work on other projects while waiting for one with Lemon?

Absolutely. As an independent contractor, you are free to accept or reject any work. When the right project comes in, we’ll be in touch.

No bidding, no negotiating, no late payments. Just the work you want exists. Let’s match you to it.
