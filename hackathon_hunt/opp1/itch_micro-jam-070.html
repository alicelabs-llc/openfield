💡 WHAT IS MICRO JAM?

Micro Jam is a bi-weekly Game Jam (game development competition) held on itch.io. Every Jam has a pre-set theme and a surprise prerequisite that is divulged once the Jam starts. The prerequisite can be voted on in the Micro Jam Discord server and is open for interpretation.

There is NO voting on itch.io. All official voting will be held on the official Micro Jam website. This is to prevent cheating. You MUST ALSO RATE AT LEAST 5 GAMES on the Micro Jam website for your game to be eligible.

It's absolutely fine if you do not finish your game in time. You can submit it late within 6 hours after the submission window closes!

▸ Special thanks to Easel and Ziva for sponsoring and making these jams possible.

🎯 RATING

When submission is over, there will be a 48 hour rating period held on the Micro Jam website (https://www.microjam.xyz/jam/70/vote). It is crucial that you rate games on the Micro Jam website, as those are the only votes that count toward prizes. Rating is disabled on itch.io, so there will be no voting there.

These are the criteria:

➤ Art - Quality, consistency, and creativity of the game's visual presentation.

➤ Audio - Quality and effectiveness of the music, sound effects, and overall audio.

➤ Polish - Attention to detail, presentation, smoothness, and overall refinement.

➤ Fun - How enjoyable, engaging, and satisfying the game is to play.

➤ Theme - How creatively and effectively the game incorporates the jam's theme.

➤ Prerequisite - How well the jam's prerequisite has been incorporated.

▸ After the voting period on the Micro Jam website has ended, a judge panel will assess a bracket of games to be chosen for a prize (see "Prizes" section above).

▸ The top 10 games rated on the Micro Jam website will be judged by our judges, and all games in the special categories (Ziva and Easel) will be judged by our judges. Judge decisions are the final placement.

📜 RULES

➤ Keep your game content clean (no explicit, NSFW, or disturbing graphics)

➤ Keep your game content in English (the title can be in any language)

➤ You can submit your game late, within the 6 hour grace period, through our Discord

➤ Do not delete or change your game files after the 6 hour grace period, since submissions will be locked

➤ All voting is done on the Micro Jam website. There is no voting on itch.io

➤ You can't win 2 prizes in the same category. Your lower ranking entry won't be considered for judging

➤ Judge decisions are the final placement and are not open to appeal

➤ To be eligible for prizes, you must be a member of our Discord community

➤ You may submit multiple games, but all submissions must come from the same account. Using more than one account to submit is not allowed

➤ Hosts reserve the right to remove or disqualify any game at their discretion if wrongdoing is suspected

➤ Don't be a jerk

➤❓ FAQ
➤ If I'm in a team, do all members have to submit the game?

No, only one person should submit it. You can add your team members to the game page.

➤ Can I submit a game I already made?

No, you can only submit games made in the given timeframe.

➤ Can I use premade assets?

Yes, as long as you have permission to use them, or they're your own.

➤ Can I use AI (artificial intelligence)?

Yes, AI is allowed.

➤ What's a prerequisite?

A prerequisite is something your game must follow. It is voted on and released once the Jam starts.

➤ Does my game have to follow the theme and prerequisite?

No, but both are part of the rating criteria, so try to integrate them into your game.

➤ Where do I vote?

All voting is done on the Micro Jam website at https://www.microjam.xyz/jam/70/vote. There is no voting on itch.io.

➤ Is there a prize for winning?

Yes, we offer numerous prizes. Check the "Prizes" section above.

➤ How are the winners decided?

The top 10 games rated on the Micro Jam website are judged by our judges for Judge's Choice, and all games in the special categories (Ziva and Easel) are judged by our judges. Judge decisions are the final placement.

➤ Can someone win 2 awards?

Yes and no. Someone can win both a top 3 award and a "Best Ziva / Easel Entry" award. Their prize includes both awards (for example, a Discord role and a CraftPix key, plus 2 certificates) and the cash prize.
