📋 How to Claim a Bounty

1

Comment on the Issue

Find the corresponding GitHub issue and comment "I'd like to work on this". A maintainer will assign it to you.

2

Submit a Plan

Within 48 hours, post a brief implementation plan as a comment. This helps prevent duplicate work and ensures alignment.

3

Implement & PR

Complete the work within the agreed timeline (usually 2-4 weeks). Submit a PR referencing the bounty issue.

4

Review & Payout

Once the PR is reviewed, approved, and merged, the bounty is paid out via Open Collective (USD) or domestic transfer (CNY).

💡 Want to Sponsor a Bounty?

Companies or individuals can sponsor bounties for features they need. Contact us at [email protected] or open a GitHub Discussion. Sponsored bounties get prioritized and the sponsor's logo appears in the project.
