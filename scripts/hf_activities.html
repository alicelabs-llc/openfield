Attend online · Activities

Every sticker, and how to earn it.

Every challenge this October earns a virtual sticker for your book. Collect enough and real ones turn up in the mail.

How it works

Sign in, show up, collect stickers.

  1. 01

    Sign in with MyMLH

    Free, and it takes a minute. Sign in and add your address for your first two stickers.

  2. 02

    Go to a Fest, or join online

    Register on a Fest’s page, or check the schedule for livestreams and challenges.

  3. 03

    Get a swag pack in the mail

    Collect three virtual stickers and we mail you a real sticker pack. Keep going for more and unlock even more swag.

Start your sticker book

Stickers and prizes will be mailed 8-12 weeks after Hacktoberfest concludes.
