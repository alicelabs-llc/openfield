They are often collected by malware, credential stealers, and other malicious software present on end-user devices.\n\nOccasionally, we may receive reports of secrets, passwords, tokens, or other credentials belonging to Canva employees, contractors, or end-users that have been leaked through developer tooling (e.g., in a GitHub commit).\n\nThese are either credentials related to a canva.com account, or API keys/secrets belonging to employee/contractor accounts on the services used to develop, build, deploy, and operate Canva.\n\nUnder no circumstances should suspected credentials be tested. Canva will handle the validation of the supplied credentials and assess their origin and impact.\n\nWe will not accept reports containing credentials that have been purchased from third parties. We operate tooling to notify us of these listings, and the proceeds from these purchases are often used to fund criminal activities.\n\nLeaked Employee/Contractor Credentials and Secrets\n\nThese reports will be assessed on a case-by-case basis. Payouts will be determined by the impact of the leaked credentials. It is important to note that Canva uses Okta with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), which means that some credentials obtained through malware or credential stealers may not be usable or may only allow access to the MFA prompt page.\n\nLeaked User Credentials and Secrets\n\nSubmissions disclosing leaked user credentials will only be accepted if the report includes technical details of the vulnerability or misconfiguration within Canva's systems which led to their disclosure. Valid reports will triaged according to the severity of the vulnerability identified.\n\nWe will NOT accept reports of leaked customer credentials that are:\n\nSourced from malware logs, credential stealers, or malicious software on end-user devices\nFound on OSINT platforms, cybercrime forums, or dark web marketplaces\nPart of publicly available \"combo lists\" or credential dumps\nNot directly tied to a vulnerability in Canva's products or infrastructure which is disclosed in the same report.\n\nCanva maintains monitoring systems for detecting compromised credentials from public sources.
