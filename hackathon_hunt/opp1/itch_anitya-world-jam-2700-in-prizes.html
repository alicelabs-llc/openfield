Who we are

Anitya is a no-code platform for creating 3D worlds. Built by the team behind Godot. Release 3.0 landed in late August, and it came with immense possibilities.

Anitya is currently in open beta; some of it is rough. That is part of why we are running this Jam: to find out where.

Prizes

First place $1000
Second place$700
Third place $400
Weekly and surprise challenges $600

Winners will be announced two weeks after submissions end. 

 Evaluation Criteria

MetricWeight
Immersive60%How interesting is your world? Would I genuinely want to play again?
Creative20%Are you creatively using Anitya's features?
Inviting20%How likely are people to get curious about the world?  Your World title and thumbnail are a good place to start.

How to enter

Everything happens in the Anitya Discord. Start there.

Join the Anitya Discord

  • Join the Discord, above
  • Make a free account at app.anitya.space
  • Build your world and publish it to the public feed
  • Submit the link to your World in our Discord

You are welcome to also put up a page here on itch so other jammers can find and play your world, but an itch submission on its own is not an entry.

Rules

  • Build it during the jam. Worlds published before 9 September are not eligible.
  • Imported assets are welcome. GLB files import directly. You must own or have the rights to anything you bring in.
  • Your world must be published to the public feed, not kept private.
  • Submitting someone else's world removes you from the jam.
  • Free to enter. 16+ to receive a prize.

Help

Questions, the weekly challenge drops, and live sessions all happen in the Anitya Discord
