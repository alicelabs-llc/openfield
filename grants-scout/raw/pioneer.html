Pioneer

Pioneer is a startup accelerator for ambitious outsiders. We've funded 150+ companies with 300+ founders in 50+ countries. We stopped making new investments in 2024.

Our companies are worth over $2B in aggregate. They've gone on to raise from Sequoia, a16z, General Catalyst, Y Combinator, Khosla Ventures, and many more.

Some of our investments include:

  • Railway (TQ Ventures, Redpoint)
  • Slash (Ribbit, Goodwater, NEA)
  • Roboflow (GV, Craft)
  • E2B (Insight, Decibel)
  • TextQL (DCM, Neo)

Pioneer was founded by Rishi Narang and Daniel Gross in 2018, and is backed by Stripe and Marc Andreessen.
