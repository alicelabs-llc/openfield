3 launches, some traffic, almost no leads. Are we doing this right?

by Arg Software

We're a small software studio trying to grow by building free tools. Here's what we're learning.

Hey Indie Hackers! 👋

We're ARG, a small software studio from Portugal. We love building software, exploring architecture, and creating free and open-source tools.

But we've discovered something: building software is much easier than getting people to notice it. 😅

We don't have a big marketing budget or an established audience, so we decided to try a different approach.

Instead of constantly promoting our services, why not create something genuinely useful and give it away?

Our first experiment: Arg Architect

We built a free browser extension that audits websites for security, performance, accessibility, architecture, and AI readiness.

It started as an internal tool we needed ourselves. No accounts, no tracking, and reports stay on your device.

Then we decided to share it with everyone.

Our launch results so far 🚀

Product Hunt: 5 upvotes. Not exactly viral! 😂 But surprisingly, we saw quite a lot of traffic coming to our website.

The problem? Apart from new LinkedIn contacts, zero new leads.

DevHunt: 4 upvotes so far. A slow start.

Smol Launch: This one's more encouraging! We're currently 2nd with 37 points, just behind the leader at 41.

We're pleased to see some support, but it raises a question: are we actually building awareness, or just collecting temporary traffic?

What's the bigger plan?

We're trying to grow ARG into a recognizable studio in the software architecture space.

We also have more products in place, including our next adventure, Fabletopia, a personalized bedtime-story app for children.

Our thinking is simple: build useful things, share them, earn people's trust, and hopefully grow a community interested in what we create next.

Maybe we're approaching this all wrong, but that's why we're here!

Could we get some support and advice? 🙏

If you'd like to check out Arg Architect or support our launches:

  • Vote on Smol Launch
  • Support us on DevHunt
  • Try Arg Architect for free

And we'd genuinely love to hear from you:

Have you successfully used free tools to build visibility for your business or future products?

Would you keep launching on these platforms or focus on building a community elsewhere?

We're a small team learning as we go, and every piece of feedback helps.

Thanks, Indie Hackers! ❤️
